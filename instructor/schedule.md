# Delivery schedule

Fundamentals is **14 meetings × 150 minutes = 2,100 minutes = 35 contact hours** over **12 weeks**. Meeting time includes a 10-minute break and active review. If you advertise 35 hours excluding breaks, add 140 minutes to the schedule instead of silently claiming both definitions.

| Week | Meetings | Topics | Preparation deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 01 | Backend and first program | Go/editor or supported machine |
| 2 | 02 | Values, types, scope | Working greeting |
| 3 | 03 | Decisions and loops | Seat arithmetic |
| 4 | 04, 05 | Functions/errors; structs/entities | Separate meetings by at least 2 days |
| 5 | 06 | Git and project shape | Git installed |
| 6 | 07 | HTTP and JSON | curl available |
| 7 | 08 | Go HTTP API | Working HTTP demo |
| 8 | 09 | Docker | Docker/Compose installed and verified |
| 9 | 10 | SQL/data model | Database starts; entity diagram ready |
| 10 | 11, 12 | Go/Postgres; tests/security | Separate meetings by at least 2 days; driver downloaded |
| 11 | 13 | Capstone workshop | Working project and gap list |
| 12 | 14 | Demos and review | Final commit and evidence |

For weeks 4 and 10, cap the first meeting’s homework at 2 hours and move remaining practice after the second meeting. Publish the two extra class dates before enrollment. An alternative is one meeting weekly for 14 weeks; that is longer than the 12-week plan.

Intermediate and advanced each use 14 weekly 180-minute meetings (42 contact hours each, including breaks). Use their lesson index order. Allow a bridge week between tiers for repairs and placement. Total core contact time is **119 hours**. The suggested sequential calendar is 40 teaching weeks plus bridge weeks/holidays; it is not a promise to complete all three tiers in three months.

## Outside the core hours

Offer an optional 60-minute weekly clinic, peer practice, and a pre-course setup drop-in. Fundamentals homework totals roughly 28–56 hours; each later tier roughly 56–84 hours. Track actual student effort and adjust. Clinics are support, not hidden mandatory syllabus hours.

## Class-size assumption

Plan for 8–12 learners with one instructor. Pair students for coding but assess individuals. For larger cohorts add an assistant or demonstration sessions: the final fundamentals meeting reserves about 60 minutes for short individual demos, enough for 12 learners at 5 minutes each. Advanced demos use pairs/teams, with individual explanation checks. Do not promise individual 10-minute demos to a large class within one meeting.
