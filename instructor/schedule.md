# Delivery schedule

Fundamentals is **17 meetings × 120 minutes + one 60-minute demonstration = 2,100 minutes = 35 contact hours** over about **12 weeks**. Meetings include active review and a 5-minute break. If your venue counts breaks outside advertised teaching hours, add the breaks to the schedule instead of silently claiming both definitions.

| Week | Meetings | Topics | Preparation deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 01 | Request/response cycle | Go/editor or supported machine |
| 2 | 02, 03 | First Go program; conditions/loops | Working greeting |
| 3 | 04, 05 | Functions/errors; data models | Working rules artifact |
| 4 | 06, 07 | Git/debugging; networks/IP/ports | Git installed |
| 5 | 08, 09 | TCP/bytes; raw HTTP | Networking tools available |
| 6 | 10, 11 | net/http server; API contract | Working server |
| 7 | 12, 13 | Routing/validation; Docker | Docker/Compose verified |
| 8 | 14 | Relational SQL | Database starts |
| 9 | 15 | Go/PostgreSQL | Driver downloaded |
| 10 | 16 | Tests/configuration/security | Working persistence |
| 11 | 17 | Final project workshop | Working API and gap list |
| 12 | 18 | Demonstration and review | Final commit and evidence |

In weeks with two meetings, cap the first meeting’s homework at 2 hours and move remaining practice after the second meeting. Publish all class dates before enrollment. An alternative is 18 weekly meetings with the final demonstration as a short review session; that is longer than the 12-week plan.

Intermediate and advanced each use 14 weekly 180-minute meetings (42 contact hours each, including breaks). Use their lesson index order. Allow a bridge week between tiers for repairs and placement. Total core contact time is **119 hours**. The suggested sequential calendar is 40 teaching weeks plus bridge weeks/holidays; it is not a promise to complete all three tiers in three months.

## Outside the core hours

Offer an optional 60-minute weekly clinic, peer practice, and a pre-course setup drop-in. Fundamentals homework totals roughly 36–72 hours; each later tier roughly 56–84 hours. Track actual student effort and adjust. Clinics are support, not hidden mandatory syllabus hours.

## Class-size assumption

Plan for 8–12 learners with one instructor. Pair students for coding but assess individuals. For larger cohorts add an assistant or demonstration sessions: the final fundamentals meeting reserves about 60 minutes for short individual demos, enough for 12 learners at 5 minutes each. Advanced demos use pairs/teams, with individual explanation checks. Do not promise individual 10-minute demos to a large class within one meeting.
