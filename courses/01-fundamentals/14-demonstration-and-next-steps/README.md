## Lesson

### 1. Assessment checks understanding and reproducibility, not the number of libraries used.

Ask students to trace a request, explain a rule, and change one small behavior live.

### 2. Errors during a demo are useful evidence if the student diagnoses them methodically.

Give a short repair opportunity and record the specific gap rather than assigning a vague label such as not technical.

### 3. The 20% milestone means introductory coverage across the local curriculum map.

It does not certify production readiness. Intermediate will deepen Go, persistent booking correctness, security, and operational behavior.

## Exit check

- Capstone rubric and evidence are recorded for each learner.
- Each learner has a concrete next practice task.
