# Advanced 14: Final demonstration and operational handoff

[Tier index](../README.md) · [Instructor notes](TEACHING.md) · [Lab](LAB.md) · [Quiz](QUIZ.md) · [Homework](HOMEWORK.md)

**Duration:** 180 minutes. **Outcome:** Demonstrate a small operated infrastructure and defend its guarantees and limitations.

Complete [Infrastructure capstone integration and rehearsal](../13-infrastructure-capstone-workshop/README.md), including its next-class preparation.

## Lesson

### 1. The final demonstration shows working behavior and recovery, not only diagrams.

Require evidence for TLS verification, authorization, concurrency correctness, durable work, and data restoration.

### 2. An operational handoff names owners, runbooks, release procedure, secrets rotation, backups, alerts, and known risks.

A system nobody can recover is not finished merely because it starts.

### 3. Completion means students can build and operate the defined small infrastructure with review.

It does not imply exhaustive backend expertise or readiness to operate every workload without supervision.

## Exit check

- Mandatory advanced safety and correctness gates pass.
- A peer can follow the operational runbook.
- Each student explains a subsystem they personally implemented.
